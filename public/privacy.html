Privacy & help / 隐私与帮助

Progress / 练习进度

Guests save answers and imported banks in this browser only. Clearing browser data, private browsing, or changing devices may lose those records. Export important progress first.
游客答案与导入题库仅保存在当前浏览器。清理浏览器数据、无痕浏览或更换设备可能丢失记录，请先导出重要进度。

Signed-in course mode saves your bank and answers under your site-specific account identity for cross-device sync. Other visitors cannot browse your answers. The site operator can technically administer the database. Demo mode is local only. Different accounts use separate local storage.
课程模式登录后，题库与答案按本站账号身份保存，可跨设备同步；其他访客无法浏览你的答案。网站运营者在技术上能够管理数据库。示例模式仅保存本机进度，不同账号的本机存储也相互隔离。

Nicknames / 昵称

Public sharing is off by default. You may choose a nickname and opt in; any visitor can then see it. Avoid real names or contact details. Nicknames are unverified and may repeat. The list shows opted-in sessions active in roughly the last 90 seconds, not total visitors.
默认不公开昵称。你可自行填写昵称并主动开启展示，开启后所有访客均可看到。请避免使用真实姓名或联系方式。昵称未经认证且可能重复，列表仅显示约 90 秒内活跃的自愿公开会话，并非总访客数。

An HttpOnly cookie identifies your nickname session. The application stores a hash of its random token, your nickname, visibility and activity timestamps. Sessions expire within 24 hours; expired rows are cleaned up when new sessions are created. Leaving the list deletes the current session. A short-lived hashed network identifier limits new-session abuse; rate-limit entries expire within one hour and are cleaned up on new-session creation. Hosting providers may maintain technical logs under their own policies.
HttpOnly Cookie 用于识别昵称会话。应用保存随机令牌的哈希、昵称、展示状态和活动时间，会话有效期不超过 24 小时，过期记录在创建新会话时清理。退出展示会删除当前会话。短期哈希网络标识用于限制新建会话滥用，限流记录一小时内到期并在新建会话时清理。托管平台可能依其自身政策保存技术日志。

Your controls / 你的控制权

Practice pages offer progress export and deletion. Signed-in deletion erases the current cloud bank and answers and this browser's associated backups. A minimal deletion marker remains to stop stale devices re-uploading old answers automatically. Offline devices may still hold local copies until they reconnect; manual exports cannot be recalled. Nickname sharing can be disabled separately. Progress exports include your answers; keep them private.
练习页提供进度导出和删除。登录后的删除操作会清除当前云端题库、答案及本浏览器关联备份，并保留最少的删除标记，以阻止旧设备自动重新上传旧答案。离线设备在重新连接前仍可能保留本机副本，手动导出文件无法收回。昵称可独立停止展示。进度导出含你的答案，请妥善保管。

Content & availability / 内容与可用性

Answers reflect the supplied course bank and may need correction. This tool is a study aid. Administrators may hide the course entrance and stop new downloads, but previously downloaded or open-source copies cannot be recalled. Availability depends on hosting quotas.
答案依据所提供课程题库，可能需要校对，本工具用于辅助复习。管理员可以关闭课程入口并停止新的题库下载，已经下载或开源的副本无法收回。服务可用性受托管额度影响。
